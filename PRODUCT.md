# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students preparing for the new TOEFL iBT (January 2026 format). They are typically 16–28 years old, non-native English speakers, often under admissions deadlines. They practice on laptops/phones at home, during commutes, or in short focused sessions. They need realistic, trustworthy feedback they can compare to official ETS scores.

## Product Purpose

A browser-based TOEFL iBT 2026 practice app. No backend — BYOK LLM scoring + native browser APIs for speech and audio. Helps users train the five current task types with official-style rubrics, timers, and band scoring so they know exactly where they stand.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credible, academic. Think ETS official prep meets Apple Notes: clean, confident, no hype. The product should feel like a serious study tool, not a startup landing page.

- Precise
- Trustworthy
- Focused

## Anti-references

- No startup SaaS clichés (big metric counters, gradient heroes, cartoon illustrations).
- No cartoon mascots or game-like rewards.
- No walls of text instructions.
- No fake "AI magic" copy.

## Design Principles

1. **Official first**: every score, timer, and rubric references the real TOEFL iBT 2026 spec.
2. **Practice over persuasion**: the landing sells by showing the actual app, not marketing claims.
3. **Clarity under pressure**: timers, scores, and next steps are instantly readable during timed tasks.
4. **Respect the user**: no dark patterns, no forced signups, API key stays local.
5. **Calibrated feedback**: scores explain *why*, not just *what*, using official dimensions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA target.
- Reduced motion support for timers and transitions.
- Sufficient contrast for all text.
- Keyboard-navigable practice UI.
