Scoring honesty

A useful estimate should admit its limits.

The live TOEFL is adaptive and statistically equated. A browser app cannot reproduce the official scoring model from a handful of practice questions.

That is why TOEFL Prep reports task accuracy and a broad practice-band estimate. Only ETS can issue a TOEFL score. AI feedback is optional and your provider key can stay in the current browser tab.

Before you begin

Common questions

Is this an official ETS product?

No. It is an independent study tool based on publicly described task families and timing. TOEFL and TOEFL iBT are registered trademarks of ETS.

Do I need an API key?

No key is needed for Reading, Listening, Build a Sentence, Complete the Words, or transcript accuracy. A key is optional for detailed evaluation of free-form speaking and writing.

Where is my progress stored?

Attempt history and your target band are stored in this browser. You can export the history as JSON. No account or project backend is required.

Will speech recognition work everywhere?

It works most reliably in a current version of Chrome or Edge on a laptop. Browser support and transcript accuracy vary, so speaking results remain approximate.
